# Ready-to-run image prompts

Paste one per message into ChatGPT. Each is self-contained. Save the PNG with
the **filename** given, drop them all in one folder, and tell me where — I do
the conversion, alt text, honesty check and wiring.

Built from `docs/art-direction.md`. Every one is category **GREEN**: a
photograph of materials, claiming nothing about who made what. No people, no
hands, no garment presented as Sam's work.

---

## What the first run taught me

`01-canvas.png` came back excellent and is already live on `/suits`. Two
corrections folded into everything below:

1. **Name the material precisely or it drifts.** I asked for "horsehair canvas
   interlining" and ChatGPT titled the result *"Rustic Linen and Wool on Wood"*.
   It read as linen. Every prompt below now names the material twice, once by
   trade name and once by what it physically looks like.
2. **State the ratio in words, not just numbers.** "Square 1:1 crop" worked.
   Bare "3:2" tends to get ignored, so the landscape prompts say
   "wide landscape crop, three units wide by two tall".

Output arrives around 1250px on the long edge, which is enough. Do not upscale.

## When they come back

Drop the PNGs anywhere and tell me the folder. I will:

1. Check each against the GREEN rule before anything else. Anything that renders
   a person, a hand or a finished garment gets rejected rather than cropped.
2. Convert to WebP at the right size: 1500x1000 landscape, 1100x1650 portrait,
   1024 square. Target under 300KB.
3. Write alt text describing only what is actually in frame.
4. Wire each to its page, rebuild, verify and deploy.
